Approvals & compliance

Checked before a trade sets foot on site.

Every site worker holds a current Construction Induction Card as a minimum. Beyond that, approvals and insurances are confirmed against the scope before work starts, not chased afterwards.

Site safety requirements, inductions and supervision remain with the principal contractor or site occupier for each job. Vortex's role is making sure the paperwork and the people are ready before the first fixing is driven.

  • Development Application / CDC (where required)
  • Construction Certificate & inspections
  • Home Building Compensation Fund (HBCF) insurance
  • Occupation Certificate
  • Asbestos awareness (pre-1990 buildings)
  • Owner-builder permit considerations
  • Construction Induction Card (White Card)
  • Working at Heights
