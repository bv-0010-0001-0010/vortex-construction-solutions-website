Services

Built to a plan, not a guess.

Four services that often overlap. Clients usually start with one and pull in the rest as the project takes shape.

01 · Residential

Residential construction & renovations

New homes, renovations and extensions, managed from first sketch through to handover.

Useful whether you're renovating a tired house room by room, adding an extension to unlock space, or building new from a cleared block. We manage design input, approvals, trades and sequencing so the program actually holds.

Typical scopeRenovations, extensions, knockdown rebuilds, new dwellings
TimeframeFrom a few weeks for a single room to several months for a full build
ApprovalsDA / CDC and Construction Certificate arranged as part of the scope where required
QuotingFree, itemised written quote before any work begins

02 · Commercial

Commercial construction & fit-outs

Shopfronts, offices and tenancy fit-outs delivered to a fixed program, with minimal disruption to trading hours.

We coordinate base-build works, partitioning, services coordination and finishes as one sequenced job, with a single point of contact from quote to practical completion.

Typical scopeShopfronts, office fit-outs, retail tenancies, small commercial builds
TimeframeTypically 2–10 weeks depending on scope and services coordination
Trades coordinatedPartitioning, electrical, mechanical, fire, joinery, finishes
ProgramFixed completion date, agreed before works commence

03 · Civil

Civil & site works

Earthworks, retaining walls, concrete and site preparation ahead of the build.

The work that has to be right before anything above ground goes in — cut and fill, drainage, retaining structures and concrete works, delivered with ticketed plant operators and sequenced to the build program.

Typical scopeEarthworks, retaining walls, drainage, concrete slabs and driveways
PlantExcavator, bobcat and roller operators to ticket
TimeframeScheduled ahead of the build program to avoid delays
ContactOne point of contact across civil and building stages

04 · Structural

Structural & outdoor works

Decks, carports, retaining and structural steel that extend a property properly, not bolted on as an afterthought.

Smaller scopes than a full build, but they still deserve proper footings, correct engineering where required, and finishes that match the rest of the property.

Typical scopeDecks, carports, pergolas, retaining walls, structural steel
TimeframeTypically 1–3 weeks depending on size and approvals
ApprovalsChecked against local council and CDC requirements before work starts
Often paired withRenovations and rear extensions
